What is Unwrap WETH?

WETH is Wrapped Ether, an ERC-20 representation of ETH backed by ETH deposited into the WETH smart contract; ethereum.org describes redemption as receiving the same amount in ETH when WETH is burned.

Unwrap WETH to ETH without swapping

Unwrapping WETH converts the ERC-20 token back into native ETH at a one-to-one contract rate before gas. It is different from selling WETH or swapping it through a liquidity pool. On Ethereum mainnet, the canonical WETH contract exposes deposit and withdraw mechanics; interfaces such as wallets and DEXs usually wrap those calls behind a simple button. Use unwrap when you need native ETH for gas, transfers, staking, or exchange deposits. Verify that you are unwrapping the correct WETH contract on the correct chain before approving or signing.

WETH unwrap fees and gas cost

There is no percentage fee built into the standard WETH unwrap action; the cost is the network gas required to execute the transaction. Gas varies by chain and congestion. Ethereum mainnet can be more expensive, while Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, and Polygon often cost less for routine transactions. The amount of ETH received should match the WETH amount before gas, assuming you use the correct wrapper contract. If an app quotes a poor exchange rate for WETH to ETH, it may be routing a swap rather than a direct unwrap.

Unwrap WETH on Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, or Polygon

WETH exists on multiple networks, and each network has its own contract address and unwrap process. You cannot unwrap Ethereum mainnet WETH on Base or Arbitrum unless the WETH is already on that network. First confirm the network in your wallet, then confirm that the WETH balance and destination ETH are on the same chain. On many Layer 2s, native ETH is also used for gas, so unwrapping can be useful if your wallet has WETH but not enough ETH to perform later transactions.

Why WETH will not unwrap in a wallet

A failed unwrap can come from the wrong network, insufficient native gas, interacting with a fake WETH token, a paused or unsupported interface, or a transaction reverted by the contract call. Check the token contract first, then make sure the wallet has enough native gas on that same chain. If the wallet interface fails, advanced users can use a trusted DEX interface or the verified block explorer contract interaction page. Never use a random support link or contract sent through direct messages.

Unwrap WETH FAQ

Do I lose money when I unwrap WETH?

A direct unwrap should return 1 ETH for 1 WETH before gas. You still pay the chain's transaction fee in native gas, so your wallet balance will be lower by that fee. If an interface shows a different exchange rate, verify whether it is performing a market swap instead of a direct contract unwrap.

Can I pay gas with WETH after unwrapping?

You pay gas with native ETH, not WETH. That is one of the main reasons users unwrap. If all your balance is in WETH, you may need a small amount of native ETH to submit the unwrap transaction. Some wallets or gas-relay systems can help, but standard Ethereum transactions still require native gas.

Is unwrapping WETH taxable?

Tax treatment depends on jurisdiction and personal circumstances. Technically, unwrapping changes the token representation from WETH to ETH without changing the underlying ETH amount before gas. Some tax tools may classify it as a wrap or unwrap event rather than a sale. Keep transaction records and consult a qualified tax professional for local treatment.

Can I unwrap WETH sent from an exchange?

Yes, if the exchange sent real WETH to a wallet you control on a supported network. Confirm the network and contract address first. If the exchange sent native ETH, there is nothing to unwrap. If it sent a bridged or chain-specific WETH version, you must unwrap on that same chain using an interface that supports that WETH contract.

Why does OpenSea or a DEX leave me with WETH?

NFT bids and many DeFi routes use WETH because smart contracts can transfer ERC-20 tokens through approvals. If a bid is canceled, a trade is partially completed, or you receive WETH from a sale, the funds may remain in WETH until you unwrap them. That is normal; the WETH can usually be unwrapped to ETH on the same chain.

Can I unwrap WETH on Etherscan?

Advanced users can interact with the verified WETH contract on Etherscan and call the withdraw function, but wallet and DEX interfaces are easier for most users. If using Etherscan, confirm the exact canonical WETH contract, connect the correct wallet, enter the amount in the expected units, and make sure you are on the intended network.
