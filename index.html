What is Unwrap WETH?

WETH is Wrapped Ether, an ERC-20 representation of ETH backed by ETH deposited into the WETH smart contract; ethereum.org describes redemption as receiving the same amount in ETH when WETH is burned.

How Unwrap WETH works

  1. Open the appLaunch WrapETH and connect the wallet that holds WETH on the network you want to use.
  2. Select the directionChoose WETH to ETH, enter the amount, and confirm that the token address matches the network.
  3. Keep gas availableYou need native ETH in the wallet to pay the transaction fee, even when redeeming WETH.
  4. Confirm withdrawalReview the wallet prompt, submit the transaction, and wait for the ETH balance to update.

Before you unwrap

Direct contract action

Redeeming through the WETH contract calls withdraw(), so there is no swap price route or slippage; only network gas applies.

Verify the contract

On Ethereum mainnet, the canonical WETH9 contract is 0xC02a...56Cc2.

Native ETH result

After redemption, your WETH balance decreases and your ETH balance increases, making the funds usable for gas and native ETH payments.

Unwrap WETH FAQ

How do I unwrap WETH?

Open WrapETH, connect your wallet, choose WETH to ETH, enter the amount, and confirm the withdraw transaction in your wallet.

Does unwrap WETH use a swap?

A direct WETH redemption is not a market swap. It calls the WETH9 withdraw function, burns WETH, and returns ETH, with gas as the main cost.

Why do I need ETH to unwrap WETH?

Ethereum transaction fees are paid in native ETH, not ERC-20 WETH, so the wallet needs a small ETH balance before it can submit the transaction.

Can I unwrap WETH on other networks?

WETH exists on multiple networks, but contract addresses differ. Use the matching network in your wallet and verify the token on that chain's explorer.

What is the Ethereum contract for unwrap WETH?

The canonical Ethereum mainnet WETH9 contract is 0xC02aaA39b223FE8D0A0e5C4F27eAD9083C756Cc2.
