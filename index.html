What it is

What it means to unwrap WETH

To unwrap WETH is to turn the ERC-20 token back into the network's native coin. You call withdraw() on the WETH contract with an amount; the contract burns that much WETH from your balance and sends you the same amount of ETH.

Why the two exist

Native ETH is not an ERC-20 token, so many smart contracts and DEXs cannot handle it directly. WETH is a wrapper that behaves like any other token. Unwrapping is the reverse step, and it is always 1:1.

Which chains

The interface works on Ethereum and on L2s with a canonical WETH: Arbitrum, Optimism and Base. On each chain it uses that chain's own WETH contract, so check the network in your wallet before you sign.

Polygon PoS

Be careful here. On Polygon PoS the token called "WETH" is bridged ETH, not a wrapper around a native coin, and it cannot be unwrapped to native ETH or POL with this method. To move it back to Ethereum you need the bridge, which is a different process.

When to unwrap

  • You need native ETH to pay gas.
  • You are sending to someone who expects ETH, such as an exchange deposit address or a friend.
  • An app or contract asks for native ETH rather than WETH.
Unwrap vs swap

Unwrapping vs selling WETH on a DEX

Both end with ETH in your wallet, but they work differently. Unwrapping is a contract call at a fixed rate. Selling is a market trade priced by liquidity pools.

Which to use

If the goal is just ETH, unwrapping is the direct route: you receive exactly what you burn. A DEX only makes sense when you want a different token, not when you want the same asset back.

How to unwrap

How to unwrap WETH

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and select the network that holds your WETH: Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism or Base.
  2. Enter the amount of WETH to unwrap, or use your full balance.
  3. Review the amount you will receive, which equals the amount you enter, and the estimated gas.
  4. Sign the withdraw() transaction in your wallet. No approval step comes first.
  5. Wait for confirmation. Your WETH balance drops and your native ETH balance rises by the same amount, minus gas.

Keep a little ETH

Gas is paid in native ETH, taken from the same wallet. If your wallet holds only WETH, you cannot sign the transaction, so send a small amount of ETH there first.

Fees & gas

What unwrapping WETH costs

Gas only

Unwrapping is a single transaction, so the only cost is the network gas fee, paid in native ETH. There is no protocol fee, no pool fee and no spread. See how gas works.

No approval, no slippage

The WETH contract burns tokens from the caller's own balance, so no allowance transaction is required. The rate is fixed, which means slippage and price impact do not apply.

Gas varies by chain

Gas is usually higher on Ethereum mainnet than on L2s such as Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, and it moves with network demand. Your wallet shows the estimate before you sign.

Security

Staying safe when you unwrap

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface prepares the call; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Check that the network in your wallet matches the chain you intend to use.
  • Verify the WETH contract address for that chain against the chain's official docs; lookalike tokens exist.
  • Confirm the transaction is a withdraw call for the amount you entered and sends nothing else.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero, even for widely used ones.
FAQ

Unwrap WETH FAQ

How do I unwrap WETH to ETH?

Connect your wallet, pick the network, enter an amount and sign a call to withdraw() on that chain's WETH contract. The contract burns your WETH and sends back the same amount of native ETH.

Is unwrapping WETH always 1:1?

Yes. The WETH contract returns exactly the amount of ETH that corresponds to the WETH it burns. You only lose the gas fee for the transaction.

Do I need to approve WETH before unwrapping?

No. withdraw() burns WETH from the caller's own balance, so no allowance is needed. That is one fewer transaction than a DEX sale, which usually requires an approval first.

Which networks can I unwrap WETH on?

Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, each using its own canonical WETH contract. Make sure your wallet is on the network that holds your WETH.

Can I unwrap WETH on Polygon PoS?

Not with this method. The WETH on Polygon PoS is bridged ETH, not a wrapper with a withdraw() that returns native ETH or POL. To get ETH you would bridge it back to Ethereum.

Is it cheaper to unwrap WETH or sell it on a DEX?

Unwrapping, in most cases. It costs gas only, while a DEX sale adds gas for a possible approval, a pool or aggregator fee, and slippage. Both can end with ETH in your wallet, but only unwrapping is a fixed 1:1 exchange.
